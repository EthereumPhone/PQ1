PQ1 firmware update

For PQ1 development units. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1 Choose the update file

2 Connect your PQ1

Plug it in and unlock it with your PIN first. Then click Connect and pick “PQSigner OS”.

3 Install

The update goes into the half of the PQ1’s memory that is not running. If anything goes wrong — you cancel, the cable comes out, the computer sleeps — the firmware you had keeps running, and you can simply try again. After installing, the PQ1 restarts and shows 8 words; check them against the words listed above, then enter your PIN. If a new version cannot reach the PIN screen, the PQ1 goes back to the previous version by itself after three restarts.
