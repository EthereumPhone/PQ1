Lean 4.22's `whitespace` (`Lean/Parser/Basic.lean:572-576`) does two things differently. For a plain `/-`, it skips the character right after `/-` before scanning. For `/--` and `/-!`, it reads those three characters as a token and scans from `i + 3`. Two examples show the mismatch:
  - In `/-/- -/ …`, the scanner counts the second `/-` as a nested comment and runs the comment past where Lean ends it.
  - In `/--/ -- -/ …`, the scanner ends the comment at offset 4. It then sees `--` as a line comment and blanks out real code.
