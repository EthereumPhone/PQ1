**Verdict: revise before adopting the recommendation.** Source-level review at `c75b0d9c`; no files modified or execution tests run.

1. **Critical—deposit substitution defeats the delay.** Companion supplies a vault with attacker-controlled safe; owner deposits; attacker presents the companion-held recovery code and sweeps immediately. The [draft’s deposit check](/home/nicola/.claude/projects/-home-nicola-repos-PQSigner-OS/7844318a-f4f1-49a2-b794-31ae48f548cc/research/report/13-onchain-policy.md:75) needs pinned factory/init-code identity, exact constructor parameters, chain, controlling wallet, positive delay, and actual deposit destination. Reject safe=wallet/vault/zero. Verify the safe against an independently obtained address and verify the recovery backup’s commitment. Offline address recomputation can authenticate a deterministic construction; it cannot establish safe custody, live recovery state, or backup availability.

2. **High—“recover sends everything” can permanently fail.** A token reverts, consumes gas, or blocklists the safe; an atomic sweep rolls back, potentially including the recovery latch, leaving malicious withdrawals executable. These are explicitly [undesigned cases](/home/nicola/.claude/projects/-home-nicola-repos-PQSigner-OS/7844318a-f4f1-49a2-b794-31ae48f548cc/research/report/13-onchain-policy.md:103). Fix: a constant-cost terminal recovery transition with no external calls; thereafter permissionless, independent asset sweeps exclusively to the safe. Invalidate withdrawals without iterating queues. Handle reentrancy, false-returning tokens, transfer fees, rebases and later deposits. A permanently blocklisted safe remains unfixable under immutable-destination semantics.

3. **High—an existing wallet freeze decision is omitted.** Use another slot to remove exhausted key K; bootstrap re-adds K at a fresh index with fresh counters. Removal deletes membership, while addition checks only current membership: [PQMultiOwnable.sol:175](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/contracts/smart-wallet/src/PQMultiOwnable.sol:175), [:184](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/contracts/smart-wallet/src/PQMultiOwnable.sol:184), [:242](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/contracts/smart-wallet/src/PQMultiOwnable.sol:242). Per-index monotonicity survives; the intended per-key interpretation of [invariant 7](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/CLAUDE.md:28) does not. This is already recorded as [SOL9](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/docs/security/adversarial-review/onchain-contracts-adversarial-review.md:25). Enforcing lifetime key budgets requires tombstones or per-key accounting—a wallet change. It is not a demonstrated vault-delay bypass, but Option A cannot settle it.

4. **High—wallet-mediated rescue can disappear.** One mis-signed execute can set `newOffchainCount = 65536 − post-validation slotUses`, exhausting that slot. A batch can simultaneously announce a withdrawal and drain hot funds; self-calls, including batched removal, are forbidden. [PQSmartWallet.sol:264](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/contracts/smart-wallet/src/PQSmartWallet.sol:264), [PQMultiOwnable.sol:220](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/contracts/smart-wallet/src/PQMultiOwnable.sol:220). Bootstrap ordinarily restores availability; a seed-holder can consume its remaining budget through failed duplicate registrations, then remove all slots. [PQSmartWallet.sol:342](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/contracts/smart-wallet/src/PQSmartWallet.sol:342), [:359](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/contracts/smart-wallet/src/PQSmartWallet.sol:359). Recovery therefore needs a tested direct-code path with independently funded gas.

5. **High—unspecified announcements can become standing drain authority.** If “withdraw everything” or unreserved amounts are permitted, attacker queues while empty, waits, then immediately drains later deposits. Spam can also exhaust a bounded queue or make cleanup unaffordable. The [announcement proposal](/home/nicola/.claude/projects/-home-nicola-repos-PQSigner-OS/7844318a-f4f1-49a2-b794-31ae48f548cc/research/report/13-onchain-policy.md:59) needs immutable asset/recipient/amount, reservations against existing balances, unique consumed IDs, expiry, and recovery independent of queue size. Restrict vault outflows to transfers; arbitrary calls or approvals recreate bypasses. A correctly checked positive delay prevents announce-and-execute within one batch.

6. **Medium—preimage recovery is publicly reusable authority.** A mempool observer copies the code and front-runs recovery; destination remains safe, but every vault/chain sharing that code becomes forcibly recoverable, permanently after disclosure. Domain-separating hashes does not fix reuse of the revealed preimage. Use independent codes and terminal, repeatable safe-only sweeping. The [“not a key” wording](/home/nicola/.claude/projects/-home-nicola-repos-PQSigner-OS/7844318a-f4f1-49a2-b794-31ae48f548cc/research/report/13-onchain-policy.md:67) understates a bearer secret. Random 256-bit SHA-256/Keccak commitments are reasonable; generic quantum preimage search has approximately 128-bit query complexity. [Quantum analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09383).

7. **High residual—equal keys provide no recovery priority.** Attacker announces; owner reacts near maturity; attacker’s execution lands first, or a reorg removes recovery. The [T1 guarantee](/home/nicola/.claude/projects/-home-nicola-repos-PQSigner-OS/7844318a-f4f1-49a2-b794-31ae48f548cc/research/report/13-onchain-policy.md:65) requires independent monitoring, early submission and finality margin. Cancellation cannot distinguish owner from thief. T3 also fails if coercion obtains safe control. No guardian-free contract removes these assumptions.

Protecting wallet balances, approvals, signatures and prefund remains a [launch-time decision](/home/nicola/repos/PQSigner_OS/docs/security/vendor-signing-key-compromise.md:136).
